How to review this deck

What you're looking at

This is the final visual design of the Viśvasaṃvid website, ready for your approval. Each page is shown at both desktop and mobile widths so you can judge how the site adapts. Sample content — paper titles, authors, editorial bios — is placeholder text for review; real content will replace it after sign-off.

The design system section at the top shows our palette, typography, and reusable building blocks. Then two galleries — the public site and the editorial admin panel — walk through every screen with a note on the intent behind each one.

To approve or request changes

  1. Review the design system and every screen at your own pace.
  2. Note any changes you'd like — copy, colour, layout, or feature — with the screen name.
  3. Reply on email or WhatsApp with Approve or your list of change requests.

Approving unlocks the next phase: connecting the design to your backend and going live.

Spacing

An 8-pt rhythm. Sections use generous vertical padding — 80–112px on desktop, 48–64px on mobile — so long pages breathe and cards never feel cramped.

Motion

Subtle fade-ups on scroll (150–400ms, ease-out); hover lifts on cards; animated underline on nav. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Ornament

A thin saffron accent rule under headings, a soft radial glow on hero surfaces, and light kalash-inspired dot ornament on brand bands — restrained, never busy.

Component gallery

Every reusable primitive — buttons, cards, badges, form fields — rendered on the internal Style Guide page. The team can add new pages by composing these instead of one-off code.
